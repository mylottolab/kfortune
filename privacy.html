Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026 · Weave App Solution (Republic of Korea)

Your birth data stays on your device

There are no accounts. The name, birth date and time, gender and birthplace you enter are stored only on your device, and all Saju calculations run on your device. Nothing personal is sent to our servers while you use the free features.

When you pay

We collect your email (receipt, restoring purchases, refunds), a summary of whose reading you bought (calendar type, birth date, birth time and gender — not the name), a random device ID, and the order details (order number, product, amount, method, time, transaction ID). We keep these for 5 years as required by Korean e-commerce law. We never receive card numbers.

Processors and international transfer

KG Inicis (cards), PayPal (PayPal payments), Apple and Google (in-app purchases), Supabase Inc. and Render Services Inc. in the United States (database and server hosting). Data is transferred over the network when you make a payment.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your purchase records at any time, except payment records we must keep by law. Data on your device is removed when you delete profiles in “Me & Family” or clear your browser data. We use no advertising trackers or third-party analytics.
